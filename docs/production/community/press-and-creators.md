## Creator list — 200 creators (OPS-0120)
**Segments:** horror let's-players · indie showcase channels ("hidden gems", demo round-ups) ·
handheld/DS-era nostalgia · medical/simulation players · dark-fantasy/lore channels.
**Sizes:** S < 10k, M 10k–100k, L 100k–1M, XL > 1M (target mix: 80 S, 80 M, 35 L, 5 XL).
**Languages:** same mix as press. **Columns:** channel · platform (YouTube/Twitch/TikTok) · language ·
segment · size · avg views of last 10 videos · plays demos? · contact route (business email / key
platform) · wave · status · video link.

## Key platform (OPS-0121)
Use one verification platform (Keymailer, Lurkit or Woovit — compare fees and reach at signup) for
creator requests: it verifies channel ownership and audience, and tracks delivery and coverage per
recipient. Demo access and full-game keys are both distributed through it; direct emails only for press.

## Key policy (OPS-0122)
- Every Steamworks key request is logged: date, purpose (press / creator / curator / QA / contest /
  partner), recipient, batch id, sent, redeemed, coverage.
- Keys go only to verified outlets/creators (key platform verification, or a press email on the
  outlet's domain). **No keys to unverified requesters** ("I'm a streamer, send 5 keys").
- Batches are small (≤ 50) and purpose-specific; unused keys from a batch are **revoked** after 30 days;
  a key found on a reseller is revoked and its recipient blacklisted.
- Two people can request keys in Steamworks; the log lives in the private CRM.
