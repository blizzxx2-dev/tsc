**What is Suture & Steel?**
A surgery-action game in a grim early-modern fantasy city. You operate on patients with period
instruments against the clock, and face the Malison — a living curse — inside them.

**Which platforms?**
PC via Steam: Windows and Linux. Steam Deck is supported (we are testing for a "Playable" or better
rating). Other platforms are not announced.

**Does it work with a controller / on Steam Deck?**
Mouse and keyboard is the primary control scheme. Controller basics and Steam Deck support are part
of the demo; full controller support is planned for launch.

**Which languages?**
The demo: English, German, French, Spanish (Spain), Polish and Brazilian Portuguese (interface and
subtitles). More languages — including Italian, Japanese and Korean — are planned for the full game.
Voice acting is English.

**When does it release?**
The free demo arrives for Steam Next Fest in February 2027. The full game is planned for 2027 —
wishlist it on Steam to be notified.

**Will my demo progress carry over?**
We intend to carry demo saves over to the full game and will confirm before the demo launches.

**Is it accessible?**
Options include a more generous timer, a key to invoke the Litany instead of drawing it, reduced
flashing and screen-shake settings, with more accessibility options planned.

**How gory is it? Content warnings?**
The game shows detailed surgical gore (open wounds, blood, pus, parasites), occult and religious horror
themes, and patients can die. There is no sexual content. A reduced-gore option is planned.

**Can I stream or make videos?**
Yes, including monetised videos. The music is cleared for streaming.

**Is it based on another game or franchise?**
No. Suture & Steel is an original game with its own world, characters and story.
