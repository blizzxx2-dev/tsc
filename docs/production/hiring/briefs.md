## Narrative editor (OPS-0043) — contract by 30 Oct 2026
Archaic-register English editor for the NAR proofread and continuity passes on Chapters I–II
(≈ 2,400 words now; ≈ 20,000 for the full game). **Paid sample edit:** the Prologue (10 lines,
`loc/export/content.en.xliff`, ids `prologue.*`) with tracked changes and a one-page style note.

Agree Chapter III–V availability (≈ Apr–Jul 2027) in the contract.
