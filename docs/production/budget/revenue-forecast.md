# Revenue forecast and wishlist targets (OPS-0036, OPS-0098)

Owner: Owner. Re-run after Next Fest (March 2027) with real wishlist and demo data, and at the Beta
pricing decision (OPS-0134).

## Assumptions
| Input | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | USD 19.99 | Proposed (D-0018) |
| Realised price after regional pricing and launch discount | 75 % of list | Valve's regional table lowers most non-US prices; 10 % launch discount |
| VAT / sales tax share withheld | 10 % | Blended across regions |
| Refunds | 8 % | Genre-typical for a 6–8 h narrative game |
| Steam revenue share | 30 % | Drops to 25 % above USD 10 M — not reached in any scenario |
| **Net per unit** | **USD 8.69** | 19.99 × 0.75 × 0.90 × 0.92 × 0.70 |
| First-year units per launch wishlist | 0.25 / 0.40 / 0.60 | Conservative / base / optimistic, including sales after launch |
| Total budget (Demo + 1.0, external spend incl. contingency) | USD 312,030 | [README.md](README.md); excludes team labour |

## Scenarios
| Scenario | Wishlists at launch | First-year units per wishlist | First-year units | Net revenue (USD) | Result vs total budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 20,000 | 0.25 | 5,000 | 43,448 | −268,581 |
| Base | 50,000 | 0.40 | 20,000 | 173,793 | −138,236 |
| Optimistic | 120,000 | 0.60 | 72,000 | 625,655 | +313,626 |

**Break-even:** 312,030 / 8.69 ≈ **35,900 units** in year one, which implies **≈ 90,000 wishlists at
launch** at the base conversion (≈ 144,000 at conservative conversion). Team labour raises the bar
further. The target is therefore set at the break-even level, not the base scenario, and the budget
should be re-cut if the Next Fest result makes 90,000 implausible (below 30,000 wishlists after the
festival).

## Monthly wishlist targets (OPS-0098)
Cumulative wishlists at month end, from the announce (17 Nov 2026) to launch (21 Oct 2027), shaped
around the events that drive wishlists (announce, Next Fest, summer showcases, release-date reveal,
launch). Tracked every Friday in the status note.

| Month end | Target | Driver |
|---|---:|---|
| Nov 2026 | 3,000 | Announce, trailer, press release |
| Dec 2026 | 6,000 | Social cadence, holiday sale visibility |
| Jan 2027 | 9,000 | Press preview build, creator outreach |
| Feb 2027 | 25,000 | Demo public 15 Feb, **Next Fest** 22 Feb – 1 Mar |
| Mar 2027 | 28,000 | Post-fest demo update |
| Apr 2027 | 31,000 | Dev updates, showcase applications |
| May 2027 | 34,000 | Showcases |
| Jun 2027 | 40,000 | Summer showcases, Steam Summer Sale traffic |
| Jul 2027 | 45,000 | Themed Steam events with the demo |
| Aug 2027 | 55,000 | Release-date reveal, Gamescom-period coverage |
| Sep 2027 | 70,000 | Previews, launch trailer |
| Oct 2027 (launch) | 90,000 | Launch |

**Below-target actions** (applied in the week a month-end or weekly pro-rata target is missed by
> 15 %): 1) check the store funnel (impressions → visits → wishlists) and run the capsule/short
description test (OPS-0088); 2) add one creator wave from the creator list; 3) schedule an extra
Steam event or showcase entry; 4) if two consecutive months miss, revisit the revenue forecast and budget.
