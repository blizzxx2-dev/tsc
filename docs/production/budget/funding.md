## Options
| Route | What it gives | What it costs | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-funding** | Full ownership, IP and creative control; no approvals | Cash risk sits with the owner; smaller marketing push | Good to Next Fest (Demo phase USD 110k) |
| **Publisher** | Recoupable advance (typically covers remaining development + marketing), PR/QA/loc/porting services | Revenue share (often 30–50 % after recoup), approvals, possible IP/sequel rights asks | Strongest after Next Fest data; negotiate from wishlist evidence |
| **Regional games funds** (e.g. national/regional development or production funds, creative-industry grants) | Non-dilutive money, often 30–50 % of eligible costs | Application windows, co-financing, spend-in-region rules, reporting | Depends on the company's country — list the funds for it below |
| **Private investment** | Cash for equity | Dilution, investor reporting | Only if a multi-project studio plan exists |

## Recommendation
Self-fund to Next Fest; prepare the pitch now; open publisher and fund conversations with Next Fest data
(March 2027). Accept a publisher only if: the IP stays with the company, the revenue share steps down
after recoup, marketing spend is committed in writing, and localisation/QA services are included at cost.
