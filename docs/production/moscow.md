# Demo MoSCoW (OPS-0025)

Decision D-0013 · Owner: Owner · Published to the team at the S1 planning. Each item is mirrored as a
board label (`moscow:must`, `moscow:should`, `moscow:could`, `moscow:wont`) on the matching roadmap
issues after the first roadmap sync.

## Must (the demo does not ship without it)
- Chapters I–II: 10 operations (op1-1…op1-5, op2-1…op2-5), story scenes, Malisons of Matins and Lauds
- All eight instruments with tutorials; the Litany of Stillness with its tutorial
- Scoring (COOL/GOOD/BAD/MISS or the counsel-approved replacement), combos, ranks, results ledger
- Options: audio, display/fullscreen, screen shake, reduce flashing, assists (time, Litany key), language
- Save + Steam Cloud; operating-theatre replay list; demo-complete wishlist screen
- Mouse + keyboard complete; controller basics; Steam Deck playable (no text below legibility floor)
- English + DE, FR, ES-ES, PL, PT-BR (D-0003)
- Opt-in telemetry with consent screen and privacy notice; crash reporting
- Performance budgets on min-spec and Deck; 0 open S1/S2
- Store and demo pages, announce + Next Fest trailers, press kit

## Should (planned; first to cut if the burn-up forces it)
- Final art for every ailment and boss phase (procedural placeholder is the fallback)
- Recorded SFX and six music cues (procedural audio is the fallback)
- Voiced assistant barks in English
- Demo carry-over of saves to the full game
- Steam achievements for the demo app (count toward nothing in 1.0)
- Russian and Simplified Chinese (stretch, D-0003)

## Could (only with spare capacity after feature lock is safe)
- Rank-chase extras: per-operation leaderboards
- Codex entries for Chapter I–II ailments
- Remappable keys beyond the defaults
- Photo mode / screenshot mode

## Won't (for the demo)
- **Challenge mode**
- **Alternate disciplines** (field triage, diagnosis, forensic/inquisition, bone-setting)
- **Full visual-novel voice-over** (barks only at most)
- Chapters III–V content or teasers beyond the demo-end screen
- Italian, Japanese, Korean
- macOS build (only if PLT greenlights it separately)
- Steam Workshop / mods
