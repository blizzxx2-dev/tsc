# Post-launch plan (OPS-0142 … OPS-0152)

Owner: Owner / Producer. Every item here is decided or executed after launch (handoff lists the ones
needing people and data).

## Patch cadence (OPS-0142)
- **Week 1:** hotfixes within **72 h** of an S1/S2 report.
- **Patch 1.1 at ≈ 4 weeks** (≈ 18 Nov 2027): QoL and balance from telemetry and reviews.
- **Then monthly** until two consecutive months pass with no S1/S2; each patch's scope set in triage.

## Free update 1 — challenge pack (OPS-0143)
Built around the NAR/ART "Unsung Hour" secret operation plus remixed Chapter I–V challenges with Steam
leaderboards; the seven operations deferred from 1.0 ([../scope-ch3-5.md](../scope-ch3-5.md)) are
candidates. Scope and schedule set at 1.1; announced with the public roadmap.

## Free update 2 — boss rush "Office of Hours" (OPS-0144)
All eight Malison hours back-to-back with its own leaderboard. **Go/no-go at month 2** on engagement:
go if ≥ 25 % of owners have finished Chapter V *or* challenge-mode weekly players ≥ 10 % of month-1 players.

## Paid DLC business case (OPS-0145)
The NAR "Ninth Office" chapter: cost it with the ART DLC template, loc (all 1.0 languages, LOC-0109),
VO; price USD 7.99–9.99; attach-rate assumption 12–18 % of owners at month 6. **Go/no-go after 3 months
of sales**: go if projected DLC net ≥ 1.5 × its cost at the lower attach rate.

## Mod policy (OPS-0146)
Content rules for Workshop items: no third-party IP (Games Workshop, Atlus, film/TV), no hateful or
sexual content, no real-person likeness without consent; takedowns via the
[IP-claim procedure](../legal/ip-claim-procedure.md); the EULA supplemental terms' mod clause
([../legal/eula-draft.md](../legal/eula-draft.md)) becomes active; one named moderation owner for Workshop
reports; tooling per PLT.

## Sales calendar (OPS-0147)
Steam seasonal sales (Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn) and Daily Deal requests; discount ladder tied to
months since launch: **month 2–3: 20 %**, **month 4–8: 30 %**, **month 9+: 40 %**; no deeper discount
in the first year except a Daily Deal. ART sale capsules scheduled two weeks before each sale.

## Monthly business review (OPS-0148)
First Monday of each month: revenue, wishlists outstanding, conversion, refunds, review trend,
playtime and completion (Steamworks + telemetry); decisions go to the decision log.

## Refund & review watch (OPS-0149)
Weekly for the first 3 months: refund rate and review score. **Refund rate above 10 %** or a **review
score drop of 5 points** in a week opens an investigation ticket (reasons, builds, regions).

## Bundles & cross-promotion (OPS-0150)
After 6 months, a Steam bundle or cross-promotion with a complementary indie (plague/dark-fantasy/sim);
partner and terms logged in the decision log.

## End-of-support plan (OPS-0151)
Decide when telemetry, Discord support and patches wind down (earliest 24 months after launch).
Draft notice: 90 days before telemetry shuts off, a Steam announcement and privacy-notice update;
telemetry endpoints removed in a final patch; data deleted per the retention schedule.

## Project post-mortem (OPS-0152)
Three months after launch: schedule vs plan (burn-up history), budget vs actual (budget model), what to
keep and what to change; published internally in `docs/production/postmortem.md`.
