# Chapters III–V scope lock (OPS-0028)

Decision D-0014 · Status: **Proposed**, to be locked at Alpha kickoff (1 March 2027) with the
post-demo re-plan (OPS-0029) · Owner: Owner, with NAR, GAM and BOS leads.

## The conflict this resolves
OPS-0028 budgets **≈ 5 operations per chapter plus two Malison hours** for Chapters III–V (≈ 21
operations). The NAR outline in `docs/roadmap/05-narrative-content.md` currently lists **28**
(Ch III: 11, Ch IV: 9, Ch V: 8). At the demo's measured cost per operation (design + content + art +
balance + loc) the extra seven operations are roughly 10–12 weeks of team time and would push Alpha
past July 2027. This proposal keeps every Malison hour and the story spine, and moves seven
side-stories into post-launch content where they have a home (free update 1, the "Unsung Hour"
challenge pack, OPS-0143).
