# Showcases, festivals, Steam events and press builds (OPS-0099 … OPS-0101)

## Showcase applications (OPS-0099)
Target **≥ 5** digital showcases/festivals that fit dark fantasy, horror or indie. Deadlines are
confirmed from each organiser's site and added to the master schedule when the call opens.
| Showcase / festival | Fit | Typical window | Deadline (confirm) | Applied | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steam Next Fest (Feb 2027) | Core | Feb | registration early Jan | — | [nextfest/plan.md](../nextfest/plan.md) |

| A dark-fantasy/RPG-focused showcase | Dark fantasy | varies | | | |

Outcomes (accepted / rejected / feedback) are logged in the table and summarised in the status note.

## Steam themed events (OPS-0100)
Join Steam's themed festivals (e.g. horror-themed and medieval/historical-themed fests, when Valve
publishes them) with the demo whenever dates fall between the demo release and launch. Before
registering, read the event's participation rules in Steamworks (eligibility, discount requirements,
asset specs) and record them in the event's issue. Candidate: the October horror fest in 2027 is
**after** launch — join it with a launch discount only if the release date allows.

## Press preview builds (OPS-0101)
- PLT produces a **watermarked press build** (recipient id burned into the corner of the screen and
  into the save file) distributed as Steam keys on a `press` branch.
- Each key is issued per recipient and logged in the key log ([../community/press-and-creators.md#key-policy-ops-0122](../community/press-and-creators.md#key-policy-ops-0122))
  with the **embargo date** (demo preview: 8 Feb 2027, embargo to 15 Feb 15:00 UTC).
- Recipients sign or accept the NDA/embargo terms in the key email.
