# Capsule and key-art brief (for ART; supports OPS-0087 and the store page)

Owner: Marketing lead → ART lead. Steam's current asset specification (sizes, safe areas, text rules)
is checked in Steamworks before production — the sizes below are the planning list.

## What the capsule must say in 3 seconds
**"Surgery" + "dark fantasy".** The test in [../store/demo-page.md](../store/demo-page.md#capsule-people-test-ops-0087)
passes only if ≥ 70 % of viewers name one of those.

## Composition
- **Subject:** Doctor Kreuzer's gloved hands with the lancet and gut thread over a candlelit table;
  the patient implied (a sheet, a wound edge) — no exposed gore in the small capsule (mature filter
  and featuring, see [../ratings/occult-market-check.md](../ratings/occult-market-check.md)).
- **Secondary:** a hint of the Malison — violet curse veins creeping over parchment — and the hourglass.
- **Palette:** candle amber, blood red, bone parchment, deep umber; one violet accent for the curse.
- **Style:** painterly with Dürer-engraving crosshatch on shadows; must read at 231×87.
- **Logo:** "Suture & Steel" blackletter wordmark (ART logo), subtitle only on large capsules.
- **Avoid:** pentagram/star in capsules and ads; skulls-with-hammers or eight-pointed stars (GW
  iconography); anything that resembles *Trauma Center* box art (clean hospital blue/white, anime faces).
