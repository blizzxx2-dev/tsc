## Description
*Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours* is a surgery-action game set in a grim, plague-ridden early-modern
city. As Doctor Kreuzer, apothecary-surgeon at a charity hospice, you perform fast, precise operations
with period instruments — pulling barbed arrows, draining wounds, lancing plague buboes and searing
parasites — while keeping your patient alive against the clock. A heretic coven weaves the Malison, a
living curse named for the canonical hours, that fights back from inside your patients. Your one
advantage, a prayer that slows time, is exactly what the witch hunters are looking for.

## History
The game began in 2026 as a question: what would a fast, arcade surgery game feel like in a
mud-and-candle world of plague doctors, guilds and witch trials? The prototype's first operation — a
tavern knife wound stitched by candlelight — became Chapter I.

## Features
- Surgery-action with eight period instruments and precise mouse-and-keyboard controls
- The Malison: multi-phase curse bosses inside the patient, each named for a canonical hour
- The Litany of Stillness: a hand-drawn prayer that slows time once per operation
- Graded actions, combo chains and ranks for every operation
- A visual-novel story across five chapters, painted in the spirit of the Old Masters
- Assists and accessibility options
