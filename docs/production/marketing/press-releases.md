## Announce
**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 17 November 2026, 15:00 UTC**

### *Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours* announced — a surgery-action game where the curse fights back

[City], 17 November 2026 — [Company] today announced *Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours*, a
surgery-action game for PC set in a plague-ridden, witch-hunting early-modern city. The game is coming
to Steam in 2027, with a free demo planned for Steam Next Fest in February 2027. The Steam page is live
today: [store link].

As Doctor Kreuzer, apothecary-surgeon at the charity Hospice of Saint Ildra, players race the clock to
save patients torn up by knives, barbed arrows, lead shot, fire and plague — using eight period
instruments, from the lancet and gut thread to the leech-pipe and cautery brand. Worse things wait
beneath the skin: a heretic coven weaves the Malison, a living curse named for the canonical hours,
that fights back from inside the patient. Kreuzer's only edge is a prayer that slows time — the very
thing the Inquisitor of the Ash Tribunal is hunting for.

**Key features**
- Fast, precise surgery with mouse and keyboard (controller and Steam Deck supported)
- Multi-phase Malison bosses, each an hour of a heretic office
- The Litany of Stillness: draw the sign to slow time, once per operation
- Graded actions, chains and ranks for every operation
- A five-chapter visual-novel story painted in the spirit of Dürer and Bruegel
