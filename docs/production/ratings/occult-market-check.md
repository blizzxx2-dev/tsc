# Occult-imagery market check (OPS-0078)

Owner: Producer with ART and Marketing. Reviewed 2026-09-25 against the demo content and store plans.

| Element | Where | Sensitivity | Territories / storefront concern | Mitigation (logged) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Five-pointed star gesture** (Litany of Stillness) | Core mechanic; drawn trail + medallion icon | A pentagram reads as occult/Satanic to some audiences; in the fiction it is a *prayer* sign | Some Middle-East storefront regions and conservative platforms; ad networks may flag "occult" creative | Draw the star **point-up** with a prayer ripple (never inverted, never inside a circle with goat/devil motifs); in ads and capsules, prefer the candle/hourglass motif over the star; keep an **alternate medallion icon** (hourglass) ready in the ART icon set |
| Curse sigils (eye, etc.) | Ailment on the body | Invented glyphs; no real occult symbols (no sigils of Lucifer, no real grimoire seals) | — | ART rule: sigils are original; checked in art review |
| Heretic coven (Hollow Choir), cantors | Story, bosses | Fictional religion; no real church doctrine or scripture | Low | NAR sensitivity brief; no real saints or scripture (LOC-0040) |
| Witch hunters, inquisitor, persecution | Story | Depicted critically; no real historical group named | Low | — |
| Canonical-hour names (Matins…Compline) | Boss names | Real liturgical hours used for antagonists could offend some Christian audiences | Low | Framed as the coven's perversion of the hours; marketing copy avoids mocking real liturgy |
| Gore + occult combined in store capsule | Capsule/key art | Storefront featuring may skip mature capsules | Steam's mature-content filter hides the game for users who opt out | Capsules show the surgeon, instruments and candlelight; no exposed gore or pentagram in the small capsule |

**Decision:** no content change needed for the demo; the capsule/ad guidance above goes into the ART
capsule brief ([../marketing/capsule-brief.md](../marketing/capsule-brief.md)) and the social kit.
