> per person in `docs/legal/nda/` (private) and logged in the table at the end.

**Parties:** [Company legal name], [address] ("Company") and [Name / company], [address] ("Recipient"),
each a "Party".

1. **Purpose.** Evaluating, testing, covering or contributing to the video game *Suture & Steel: The
   Malison Hours* (the "Purpose").
2. **Confidential Information** means unreleased builds, story, art, audio, designs, business
   information, release dates and anything marked or reasonably understood as confidential, disclosed
   by either Party. It excludes information that is public through no breach, already known without
   restriction, independently developed, or received lawfully from a third party.
3. **Obligations.** Each Party uses Confidential Information only for the Purpose, protects it with
   reasonable care, and shares it only with people who need it and are bound by similar terms.
4. **Builds and embargoes.** Builds are for the Recipient only; no streaming, recording, screenshots or
   publication before the embargo date stated in writing by the Company. Watermarked builds must not
   be shared.
5. **Feedback.** Feedback the Recipient gives may be used by the Company freely and without payment.
6. **Term.** Obligations last **2 years** from disclosure (story/plot details: until public release).
7. **Return/destruction.** On request, delete builds and materials and confirm in writing.
8. **No licence.** No rights are granted except as needed for the Purpose.
9. **Remedies.** Breach may cause irreparable harm; injunctive relief is available in addition to other remedies.
10. **Law.** [Jurisdiction]; courts of [venue].
