# Company-wide contractor agreement — OUTLINE FOR COUNSEL DRAFTING (OPS-0039)

> Counsel drafts the agreement from this outline (handoff OPS-0039). The ART and AUD contract
> checklists become discipline **schedules** to it; each engagement adds an SOW
> ([../budget/sow-template.md](../budget/sow-template.md)).

## Master terms (all disciplines)
1. **Services and SOWs** — work defined per SOW; SOW wins on scope, master wins on everything else.
2. **Work made for hire / IP assignment** — all deliverables, drafts and sketches are works made for
   hire where the law allows; otherwise the contractor assigns all rights worldwide, for the full term,
   in all media now known or later developed, on payment of each milestone.
3. **Moral rights** — waiver or agreement not to assert, **where lawful** (e.g. waiver in UK/US; in
   droit-d'auteur countries such as DE/FR an agreement on attribution and a licence to modify instead).
4. **Pre-existing materials** — listed in the SOW; the contractor grants a perpetual, irrevocable,
   royalty-free licence to use them in the game and its marketing.
5. **Confidentiality** — NDA terms incorporated ([nda-template.md](nda-template.md)).
6. **Originality warranty** — deliverables are original, do not copy or imitate third-party IP
   (explicitly: Games Workshop's Warhammer settings and Atlus/SEGA's *Trauma Center*), and contain no
   third-party material without a licence listed in the provenance log; indemnity for breach.
7. **Generative-AI disclosure** — the contractor discloses any generative-AI tool used (tool, purpose,
   which deliverable) in the delivery note; AI-generated content in final deliverables requires prior
   written approval; disclosures feed the Steam AI-content survey (OPS-0063).
8. **Credit** — credit wording and placement per SOW, recorded in the credit obligations register; the
   company may omit credit in materials where credits are not customary.
9. **Payment** — per accepted milestone ([../budget/README.md](../budget/README.md)); invoices within
   30 days of acceptance; expenses only if pre-approved.
10. **Independent contractor** status; taxes; no exclusivity unless stated.
11. **Termination** — for convenience with 14 days' notice (pay for accepted work plus work in progress
    pro rata); for breach immediately; IP in paid work transfers on termination.
12. **Portfolio use** — the contractor may show released work after public release; unreleased work only with written approval.
13. **Law and venue** — [jurisdiction].

## Discipline schedules
- **Schedule A — Art** (from the ART contract checklist): file formats, layered sources delivered,
  resolution, style bible compliance, likeness rules (no real persons), provenance log.
- **Schedule B — Audio/Music** (from the AUD checklist): stems, sample-library licences, **stream-safe
  clause** (no Content ID registration; composer's PRO registration does not block creators — OPS-0071),
  soundtrack release rights.
- **Schedule C — Writing/Editing**: style guide compliance, name register, no real scripture.
- **Schedule D — Localisation vendors**: TM/termbase ownership by the company (LOC-0055), LQA scope, NDA.
- **Schedule E — Voice actors**: performer consent for recording use, no synthetic voice cloning
  without separate written consent and payment, union terms if applicable.
