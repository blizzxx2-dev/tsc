# EULA decision and supplemental terms — DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW (OPS-0067)

> **Not legal advice and not for publication.** Decision entry D-0019 (Pending counsel).

## Recommendation
Use the **Steam Subscriber Agreement (SSA) only** for the demo and 1.0 — no custom EULA. Reasons:
- Steam already licenses the game to the player under the SSA; a custom EULA adds a click-through at
  first launch, a translation cost for every language, and little extra protection for a single-player game.
- Telemetry is covered by the in-game consent screen and the privacy notice, not by a EULA.
- There is no online multiplayer or user-generated content at 1.0.

**Revisit** if Steam Workshop/mods (post-launch, OPS-0146), community translations (LOC-0107) or
leaderboards with user names ship: those need rules of conduct and a content licence.
