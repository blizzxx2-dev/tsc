# Streaming and monetisation policy (OPS-0071)

Decision D-0020. Published on the website FAQ, the Steam page ("About" footer) and the press kit.

<!-- loc:start -->
## Streaming & videos
You are welcome to stream *Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours* and to publish videos of it on any
platform, including monetised videos and streams. You don't need to ask us. We only ask that you don't
present your video as official, and that you don't upload the soundtrack on its own. The game's music
is cleared for streaming: our composer's and sound libraries' licences allow it, and we will not
register the soundtrack with Content ID. If you ever receive a claim on gameplay footage, contact us at
[press@domain] and we will get it released.
<!-- loc:end -->
