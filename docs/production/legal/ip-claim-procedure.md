# IP-claim response procedure (OPS-0056)

**Draft for counsel review.** Applies to cease-and-desist letters, DMCA/takedown notices, platform
complaints (Steam, YouTube, social) and informal accusations of copying.

## Who
- **Receives notices:** `legal@<company-domain>` (role mailbox; Owner + Producer), the company's postal
  address, and the Steamworks partner contact. Anyone else who receives one forwards it there within
  1 working day and does **not** reply.
- **Counsel contact:** games-IP counsel (engagement letter, OPS-0048) — name, email, phone in the
  password manager's *Legal* item.

## Response SLA
| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge receipt internally, open a private `legal` issue (no public discussion) | same working day |
| Send the notice + our evidence pack to counsel | 1 working day |
| Evidence pack: the relevant archive snapshot ([independent-creation-archive.md](independent-creation-archive.md)), the IP name review, the trade-dress comparison, asset provenance logs (ART/AUD) | 2 working days |
| Counsel-approved reply or action | within the deadline in the notice, or 10 working days |
| Platform takedown affecting our store page/videos | counsel decides on counter-notice within 5 working days |

## Takedown handling
- Never delete evidence. Unpublish the disputed asset only on counsel's advice.
- If an asset is withdrawn, replace it with an approved alternative and log it in the decision log.
- Public statements only through the Owner after counsel approval.

## User-generated content (before any UGC ships: screenshot contests, Workshop, community translations)
- Register a **DMCA designated agent** with the U.S. Copyright Office (online, small fee, renew every
  3 years) and publish the agent's contact on the website — handoff OPS-0056.
- Publish a notice-and-takedown page describing how rights holders report content.
- Repeat-infringer policy: accounts with two upheld notices lose submission rights.
