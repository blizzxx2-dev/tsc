## Privacy notice — Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours

*Last updated: [date]*

### Who we are
[Company legal name], [registered address], [company number] ("we", "us") is the controller of the
personal data described here. Contact: [privacy@domain]. [EU/UK representative, if required: name,
address.]

### What this notice covers
The game *Suture & Steel: The Malison Hours* and its free demo on Steam, our website, our newsletter
and our official Discord server.

### What we collect, why, and on what legal basis
| Data | When | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gameplay telemetry** — a random install ID generated on your device, game version, operating system and hardware class, settings, and events such as operation started/finished, rank, time taken and vitals at failure | Only if you switch *Share anonymous play data* on (off by default) | Balance the difficulty, find where players get stuck, and measure performance | Your consent (you can withdraw it at any time in Options) |
| **Crash reports** — stack trace, game version, operating system, GPU/driver, the install ID, and the last 200 lines of the game's log | Only if you switch *Send crash reports* on, or confirm sending a single report | Fix crashes | Your consent |
| **Newsletter** — email address, language, sign-up date and confirmation, open/click events | When you subscribe and confirm (double opt-in) | Send news about the game | Your consent; unsubscribe link in every email |
| **Discord** — messages and profile data you post on our server | When you join our server | Community and support | Our legitimate interest in running the community; Discord's own policy also applies |
| **Support emails** | When you write to us | Answer you | Our legitimate interest / steps at your request |

We do **not** collect your name, Steam account ID, precise location, contacts, or payment details.
Purchases are handled by Valve (Steam) under Valve's privacy policy. The game has no advertising and
no advertising identifiers.

### Who processes the data for us
| Processor | Role | Location / transfer safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| [Telemetry provider] | Stores gameplay events | [EU region / SCCs] |
| [Crash-reporting provider] | Stores crash reports | [region / SCCs] |
| [Newsletter provider] | Sends the newsletter | [region / SCCs / Data Privacy Framework] |
| Discord Inc. | Hosts our community server (independent controller for its platform) | USA — Discord's terms |
We have data processing agreements with each processor. We do not sell or share personal data for
cross-context behavioural advertising.

### How long we keep it
- Raw telemetry events: **90 days**, then deleted; we keep only aggregated statistics that cannot identify an install.
- Crash reports: 90 days.
- Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days (a suppression entry may be kept so we never email you again).
- Support emails: 12 months after the conversation ends.

### Your rights
Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability,
and object to processing based on legitimate interests; you can withdraw consent at any time without
affecting earlier processing, and complain to your data-protection authority
([lead authority for the company's country]). California residents have the rights to know, delete and
correct, and not to be discriminated against for using them; we do not sell or share personal
information.

**Deleting your gameplay data:** open Options → Privacy, copy your install ID and email it to
[privacy@domain] with "Delete" in the subject, or use *Delete my data* in the same menu. We delete all
events and crash reports for that ID within 30 days and confirm by email. Switching telemetry off stops
all further collection immediately.

### Children
The game is not directed at children under 16 and its content is intended for mature players. We do
not knowingly collect data from children; telemetry and crash reports carry no identity.

### Changes
We will post changes here and, for material changes, show a notice in the game before any new
collection starts.
