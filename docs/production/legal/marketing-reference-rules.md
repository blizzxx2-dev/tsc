# Marketing-reference rules (draft for counsel approval — OPS-0051)

**Status: draft rules the team follows now; counsel approves or amends them (handoff OPS-0051).**
This checklist is attached to every store-page, press, trailer and social review. A reviewer ticks
every box or the asset does not ship.

## Never
- [ ] No "Warhammer", "Games Workshop", "Old World", "Age of Sigmar" or any GW faction, god, place,
      character or product name (the full list is §7 of `docs/research/warhammer-fantasy-tone.md`;
      `node scripts/ops/ip-scan.mjs` checks game text) — not in copy, tags, hashtags, alt text or metadata.
- [ ] No GW logos, art, miniatures, fonts or iconography (eight-pointed star, twin-tailed comet, skull-and-hammer motifs), and no fan art of GW IP in our channels.
- [ ] No Atlus or SEGA footage, screenshots, box art, logos, character art, music or sound, and no
      "Trauma Center", "Trauma Team", "Under the Knife", "Caduceus", "GUILT" or "Healing Touch" in paid
      placements, store pages, trailers, capsule art, tags or ads.
- [ ] No claims of affiliation, endorsement, sequel, "spiritual successor to <named game>" or
      "from the makers of…" unless true and contractually allowed.
- [ ] No real saints, real religious scripture, real historical persons or real hospitals presented as
      part of the fiction.
- [ ] No gameplay footage from a build older than the current store build without a "work in progress" card.

## Allowed
- [ ] Genre descriptors: "surgery-action", "grimdark", "dark fantasy", "early-modern", "plague", "witch
      hunters", "pike-and-shot", "Dürer/Bruegel/Bosch-inspired art" (public-domain artists).
- [ ] "For fans of surgery-action games" (no title named) in any channel.

## Pending counsel decision
- [ ] Whether press materials only (press release "about" paragraph, press-kit FAQ — not store pages,
      trailers or ads) may say "inspired by surgery-action games such as *Trauma Center*" as a factual,
      nominative reference. **Until counsel rules: do not name it anywhere.**
