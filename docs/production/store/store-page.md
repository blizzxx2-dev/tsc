## Short description (≤ 300 characters — this one is 254)
Scrub in as an apothecary-surgeon in a plague-ridden free city. Cut, drain, stitch and sear through
frantic operations, pull barbed arrows and cursed shards from the dying, and fight the Malison: a
living curse that fights back from inside your patients.

## About This Game
*[ART section banner: "THE HOSPICE"]*

**Kessendorf is dying, one patient at a time.** Plague smoulders in the Tanners' Rows, raiders prowl the
timber road, and the Hospice of Saint Ildra takes in everyone the city throws away. You are Doctor
Kreuzer, apothecary-surgeon, newly sworn — and the table is never empty.

*[GIF: incision → barbed arrow nicked free → stitched, with COOL popups]*

*[ART section banner: "THE OPERATION"]*

**Fast, precise surgery with a mouse.** Eight period instruments — lancet, tongs, leech-pipe, gut
thread, Saint's salve, tincture, cautery brand and scrying lens — each with its own motion. Trace
incisions, nick barbs loose before you pull, drain pooled blood, lance buboes, sear parasites and hold
the patient's vitals above the line while the hourglass runs. Every action is graded; clean chains
raise your rank.

*[GIF: vitals falling, the Litany star drawn, world slows to sepia]*

*[ART section banner: "THE LITANY"]*

**A prayer that stops the clock — and could send you to the pyre.** Draw the five-pointed sign to
invoke the Litany of Stillness and slow time for a few precious seconds, once per operation. The
Inquisitor of the Ash Tribunal would call it witchcraft.

*[GIF: the Malison of Matins splitting and rejoining under the cautery]*

*[ART section banner: "THE MALISON"]*

**Bosses that live inside your patients.** A heretic coven, the Hollow Choir, weaves living curses
named for the canonical hours — Matins, Lauds, Prime and five more. Each Malison fights back with its
own phases and tricks, and each one leaves a mark on the city.

*[ART section banner: "THE CITY"]*

**A grim story told in candle smoke and ink.** Visual-novel chapters between operations, painted in
the spirit of Dürer and Bruegel: guild politics, witch hunts, black humour, and the question of what a
healer owes a city that burns its sick.

## Feature bullets
- **Five chapters, eight Malison hours** — a surgery-action campaign in a grimdark early-modern world
- **Eight period instruments** with precise mouse-and-keyboard controls, controller and Steam Deck support
- **Graded, combo-driven operations** with ranks to chase in every case
- **The Litany of Stillness** — a once-per-operation prayer that slows time, drawn by hand
- **Assists and accessibility options** — generous timers, a Litany key, reduced flashing and gore settings
