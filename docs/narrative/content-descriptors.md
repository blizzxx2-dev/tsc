**Mature content description (demo):**
Suture & Steel is a surgery game set in a grim early-modern fantasy city. You operate on wounded and
diseased patients, so the game shows **blood and open wounds** throughout, including knife, arrow and
burn injuries, plague buboes and maggots in wounds (stylised, painterly flesh — not photorealistic).
There is **body horror**: living curses that grow inside patients, and a creature's eggs beneath the skin.
The story includes **religious persecution** — a witch-hunting tribunal that suspects the hero — and
**implied violence against prisoners**, which is never shown. A child is among the patients; no child
is harmed outside the operating table.
