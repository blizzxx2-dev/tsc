# Character arcs (NAR-0090, 0095, 0098, 0101, 0105, 0107, 0109)

Seven principals, one turning point per chapter each, with the scene (and flag) that carries it. Scene ids
are the campaign's (`src/content/chapter1.ts` … `chapter5.ts`); flags are in [flags.md](flags.md). Where a
beat branches, both sides are named. Use this doc to check a new line against where the character is.

## Dr. Kreuzer — from sworn craftsman hiding the Litany to open defiance (NAR-0090)

The spine: a surgeon who broke one oath in Weissburg and keeps a second secret in Kessendorf, until
keeping it costs more than telling it.

| Chapter | Turning point | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| I | First Litany in front of witnesses: the candles stop over Emmerich and Matins wakes. He decides to hide it. | `s1-end`, op1-5 |
| II | Chooses at the muster between the cantor's comfort and Stroh's interrogation — the first time the Litany's secret costs someone else. | `s2-4` (`cantorMercy`) |
| III | Tells the Guild why he tore up his Weissburg letters; writes Liesl's certificate (the kind lie or the true sentence). | `s3-2` (`hornchildCertificate`), `s3-9` |
| IV | Gives the verdict on a man with one heartbeat a minute; learns the Litany is a verse of the Choir's own office. | `s4-5` (`deadManVerdict`), `s4-10` |
| V | Under oath, confesses the star or denies it; refuses to let the Office end in the Precentor — "a patient, on the table". | `s5-2` (`trialAnswer`), `s5-12`, op5-9 |

## Sister Ilse — faith against what she sees (NAR-0095)

A nursing sister who keeps the ledger and counts the dead, and whose faith moves from the vows to the work.

| Chapter | Turning point | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| I | Holds the lamp while the candles stop, and says nothing to anyone. | `s1-end` |
| II | Takes Stroh's questions at the muster and answers only what she saw with her own eyes. | `s2-4` |
| III | Lies to Stroh for Kreuzer ("I was holding the lamp"); in the laundry yard, decides a tired man who will not stop may be holier than God in the hands. | Ilse side scene in `s3-end` (NAR-0097) |
| IV | Hears five far notes under the Litany; counts the saved as a longer column than the lost; will not go back to the convent. | `s4-2`, side scene in `s4-end` |
| V | Taken as the host of Vespers — she hears the evening office and wants to follow it; wakes asking after the lamps. | `s5-9`, op5-6, side scene in `s5-9b` |

## Master Haller — mentor, guilt, and the Precentor he made (NAR-0098)

A retired guild surgeon who struck his best pupil from the rolls twenty years ago and taught the Litany he
learned from him.

| Chapter | Turning point | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| I | Watches the Doctor's first operations and says "hm"; the only praise he gives. | op1-1, op1-2 barks |
| II | Absent at the muster; his letters are the Doctor's conscience. | Chapter II barks |
| III | Defends Kreuzer at the licence vote ("I will not do it twice"); hexfire takes him mid-speech; confesses the Precentor was his pupil. | `s3-9` (`guildMarks`), `s3-10`, `s3-end` |
| IV | Writes from his bed — his own hand if Terce left it to him, a clerk's if not — naming the Widow's sigil and the Litany's source. | `s4-10` (`hallerFate`, NAR-0100) |
| V | At the trial, in person with the hands he kept, or by letter; at the pyre, he stands all day where he once did nothing. | `s5-2`, `s5-end-pyre` |

Terce's rank decides his hands (A or better, `hands`; below, `scarred`); no rank kills him (NAR-0100).

## Inquisitor Stroh — chartered agent whose charter has lapsed (NAR-0101)

A Tribunal inquisitor who counts candles, keeps a ledger, and means to find the truth even when no one
has to let him any more. `strohTrust` (flags.md) decides whether he defends or prosecutes.

| Chapter | Turning point | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| I | Arrives at the hospice for the page-boy; counts eight heartbeats of stillness. | `s1-4`, op1-5 barks |
| II | Demands the cantor be kept awake for questioning; the Doctor's answer moves his trust. | `s2-4` (`cantorMercy`) |
| III | In the Doctor's chair with a rotten molar, interrogating between groans; the tooth, and how cleanly it came, earns trust. | op3-9 (`strohTooth`, `strohToothFine`) |
| IV | Learns his charter lapsed at the new year; with trust, writes his own protective warrant; without it, serves the council's. | `s4-8`, `s4-end` (NAR-0139, NAR-0143) |
| V | Testifies for the defence, objects, or enters his ledger for the prosecution; is Compline's host — or, estranged, comes down the crypt stair to arrest the wrong man. | `s5-2`, `s5-10`, `s5-12` (`complineHost`) |

## The Precentor — Aurel Vennholt (NAR-0109)

A struck-off surgeon who wants a perfect Compline for a whole city: an end to suffering by an end to waking.
A voice only, until Chapter IV; in person in Chapter V.

| Chapter | Turning point | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| I | A voice in Emmerich's chest; the first intercepted letter. | op1-5, codex `letter-one` |
| II | Lauds' antiphon; the second letter. | op2-5, `letter-two` |
| III | Named by Haller as the pupil he struck off; his Hours burn the Guildhall. | `s3-end`, `letter-three` |
| IV | Speaks through Pieter at the ninth hour and calls the Doctor home for Hollow Night. | `s4-11`, `s4-end`, `letter-four` |
| V | In person under the Tribunal court: the mirror scene ("the same hymn"), Compline sung into Stroh or the Burgomaster; then the whole Office in himself, and the Doctor keeps him alive. | `s5-10`, `s5-11`, op5-9, `letter-five` |
