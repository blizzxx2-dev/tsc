# Audio decisions

## Procedural first (2026-09)

Every event has a designed, synthesised recipe and every score cue is generated by the
adaptive procedural player. Recorded SFX, stems and VO replace designs one event at a
time through the manifest (`assets` on an event, `vo/<line id>` for voice). Consequences:

- The demo is fully audible without any recording session; recordings are upgrades, not blockers.
- "Fallback" means the dev-only generic tick for an id with neither recipe nor asset. The full
  Ch1–2 replay test asserts **0 fallback plays**, and release builds compile the tick out
  (`import.meta.env.DEV` guard in `engine.ts`).

## Music streaming vs decoded buffers (AUD-0023)

**Decision: decoded AudioBuffers with metadata loop points; no `MediaElementAudioSourceNode` streaming.**

- Gapless looping and sample-accurate stem sync require `AudioBufferSourceNode` loops started on one
  clock; media elements can't guarantee either (loop seams, independent clocks drift).
- Memory: a 2-minute operation cue as 4 stereo float32 stems at 48 kHz is 120 s × 48 000 × 2 ch × 4 B × 4
  ≈ 184 MB — over the 150 MB demo budget by itself. So stems are delivered **mono** (the stereo image comes
  from per-stem panning and the reverb send): ≈ 92 MB per operation cue. Only the current scene's music bank
  is resident (the boss bank replaces the operation bank on boss entry); with the operation SFX (≈ 30 MB) and
  VO (≈ 10 MB) banks the peak stays ≈ 135 MB. `scripts/audio-build.mjs --check` fails above 150 MB decoded.
- Today the procedural score costs no memory at all; this decision governs the recorded stems.

## Pulse chime (AUD-0092)

A modern monitor beep is anachronistic. **Decision: a small glass/brass tick on each beat**
(`sfx.heart.pulseTick`), scheduled with the heartbeat on the QRS. Audible when "Pulse tick" is on,
or automatically below 30 vitals; silent when Heartbeat is Off.

## Heartbeat timing (AUD-0014)

Beats are scheduled on `AudioContext.currentTime` 100 ms ahead from the ECG phase, landing on the
QRS spike (phase 0.21) within ±5 ms (unit-tested with jittered frames). The Litany slows the
heart with the world (the ECG phase advances at the 0.15 time scale).

## Litany music treatment (AUD-0074)

The player keeps its musical clock running during Stillness; on invoke all stems but the
Stillness stem dip −12 dB through a 1.5 kHz low-pass in 400 ms and the Stillness stem rises;
1.5 s before the end a reverse swell; on release the stems are exactly where they would have been.

## Reverb (AUD-0015)

Two synthesised impulse responses (stone operating theatre 1.3 s, chapel 3.6 s) as a shared
send from every bus; the ambience manager selects the space per location; only the active
convolver is fed (an idle one still costs CPU). Hour bells switch to the chapel for their tolls.

## Performance notes (for AUD-0142)

Measured with `AUDIO_PERF=1 npx vitest run tests/audio/perf.test.ts` (node-web-audio-api, one
core, 48 kHz): empty graph 1.5 %, theatre ambience 5 %, operation theme bed+pulse 7 %, all eight
stems 16 %, Matins all stems 12 %, three grubs + sizzle + bleed 5 %. Sustained instruments use
cached PeriodicWaves, percussion and dense click textures are pre-rendered buffers, and silent
stems are not scheduled. The Steam Deck budget must be verified on hardware.
