# Sensitivity reads — Chapters III–V (NAR-0031, NAR-0032, NAR-0033)

A human sensitivity reader must review the scenes and operations below. Script source:
`src/content/chapter3.ts`, `chapter4.ts`, `chapter5.ts` (each `StoryDef` is one scene; `OP_*` are
operations — read `diagnosis` and every `callout`). Design notes: `docs/content/ch3-5-operations.md`.

Log every note in a sheet with columns: scene/op id · line · note · accept/decline · reason · fixed in commit.

## Chapter III (NAR-0031) — flagellants, penitential self-harm, plague and class
- s3-6b and op3-8 "The Flagellant's Back": Brother Ansgar asks to keep the nails ("the pain is owed").
  Check that the self-harm is neither glamorised nor mocked; Kreuzer's reply is the only framing.
- s3-5, s3-6, s3-6b: the quarantine — raised bridges, beef on poles, the pie-man. Check that the
  jokes land on the council and folk belief, never on the plague-sick (style-guide humour rule).
- s3-1, s3-2, op3-1: a seven-year-old examined under an "inspection decree"; Kreuzer falsifies the
  certificate. Check the child is never shown in pain beyond the operating table, and that the
  decree's menace reads as institutional.

## Chapter IV (NAR-0032) — war violence; the thirsted courtesan
- s4-6 and op4-6: Margit, a camp-follower bitten by a blood-thrall, asks Kreuzer to leave the bond.
  Check her consent and agency: she chooses, the script does not moralise, and the in-op choice
  (brand or salve the channel) is presented as hers.
- s4-1 … s4-end: Mauer's roll-call (41 → 35). Check the deaths are counted with weight, not as a gag.

## Chapter V (NAR-0033) — obstetrics, the trial, assisted death
- s5-8 and op5-4 "Under the Hollow Moon": a caesarean on a living mother. Needs the obstetric
  consultant too (see `docs/handoff/CON/obstetric-signoff-op5-4.md`).
- s5-2, s5-3: the trial. No witch-pricking or torture is shown; check that it stays implied.
- s5-10 … s5-12: the Precentor's "perfect end" and Stroh's "let it end in him". Check that the
  assisted-death theme is argued, not endorsed or caricatured; the mirror scene never gives the
  Precentor more than four consecutive lines.
