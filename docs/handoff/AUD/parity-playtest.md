# Sound-off playtest script (AUD-0117)

Tester: deaf or hard-of-hearing player, recruited through an accessibility playtest service
(e.g. AbleGamers Player Panel), paid for their time.

Setup: demo build, **Sound → Volume → Sound: Off**, **Captions → Sound captions: On**,
**Comfort → Heartbeat: Off** (the screen-edge pulse takes over). Record screen and a think-aloud.

1. Play Chapters 1–2 start to finish, including both Malison fights.
2. After each operation, ask: Did you know when the patient was failing? When time was short?
   When the Malison was about to strike, rejoin or sing? When the Litany was ending?
3. Note any death or MISS the tester attributes to missing information.

Pass: the tester completes Ch1–2 and reports no hidden information. File every gap as a UIX task
(the audit's known gaps are A–C in `docs/audio/parity-audit.md`).
