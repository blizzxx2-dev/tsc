# Tester pool screener (QAT-0116)

Recruit **≥ 60 opted-in testers with signed NDAs** (OPS NDA template) before playtest round 1. Build the form in the
team's survey tool (Google Forms / Tally / Typeform); export responses to the "QA tester pool" sheet with the columns
below. Keep personal data minimal and delete it 6 months after the last round the tester joins.

## Form

1. **Name or handle** (short text) — used in the NDA and Steam key mail only.
2. **Email** (email) — for invitations.
3. **Steam account name** (short text) — for Playtest / private branch access.
4. **Country and time zone** (dropdown) — scheduling moderated sessions.
5. **Age bracket** (18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45+) — must be 18+ (gore and violence; see the content survey).
6. **How often do you play games?** (daily / weekly / monthly / rarely)
7. **Genres you enjoy** (multi-select): action, puzzle, visual novels, rhythm, simulation, horror, strategy, RPG.
8. **Have you played Trauma Center / Trauma Team (DS/Wii)?** (none / one / several / all) — we want a mix: ~⅓ veterans, ⅔ new.
9. **Other surgery / precision-mouse games** (short text).
10. **Hardware**: OS (Windows 10 / 11 / SteamOS Deck / Linux / macOS), CPU, GPU (free text), RAM, monitor resolution
    and refresh rate, **input you'd use** (mouse, trackpad, trackball, pen tablet, controller, Deck).
11. **Languages you'd play in** (multi-select of the planned demo languages) and **native language**.
12. **Accessibility needs** (optional, free text): e.g. one-handed play, colour-vision deficiency, motion sensitivity,
    photosensitivity — used to invite people to the accessibility round and to adapt sessions.
13. **Would you be willing to be recorded (screen + voice) in a moderated session?** (yes / no / screen only)
14. **Consent**: "I agree to the NDA and to Suture & Steel recording gameplay telemetry and optional replays during
    playtests" (required checkbox, links to the NDA and privacy notice).

## Selection

Aim per round for: 50 % Windows mouse players, 20 % Steam Deck, 10 % trackpad/trackball, 20 % other; 30 % *Trauma
Center* veterans; at least 2 native speakers per demo language; at least 3 players with accessibility needs.
