## Checklist

- ☐ Every shot comes from the tagged update build, recorded as a replay.
- ☐ New art has passed the art QA checklist before capture.
- ☐ No spoilers past the update's first boss form.
- ☐ The price is never in the video (it varies by region). The store page carries it.
- ☐ Cut-downs: 30 s (beats 2, 3, 5, 7, 8) and 15 s (beats 3, 7, 8).
