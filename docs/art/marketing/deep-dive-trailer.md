# Gameplay deep-dive trailer — 2–3 min storyboard (ART-0339)

woodcut callouts naming each instrument and what it does. It is cut from unedited replays
([capture-list.md](capture-list.md)), with the HUD on. Callouts use the in-game title-card style (a
black band, Cinzel capitals, a gilt rule), in the top third, and each stays up at least 2.5 s.
