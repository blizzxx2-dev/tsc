# Accolades trailer template (ART-0340)

A 45–60 s re-cut of the launch trailer with press quotes and awards, made after launch. This page
is the layout template. The quotes are filled in once the reviews exist.

## Structure

| # | Time | Picture | Accolade overlay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | Cold open from the launch trailer (hands in the basin) | — |
| 2 | 0:05–0:12 | Chapter I–II montage | Quote 1 (the strongest single line) |
| 3 | 0:12–0:20 | Litany star and sepia | Laurel 1 |
| 4 | 0:20–0:28 | Later Hours (first forms only) | Quote 2 |
| 5 | 0:28–0:36 | The city at night | Quote 3 |
| 6 | 0:36–0:44 | Rank seal "XS" | Laurel row (up to 4) |

| 8 | 0:52–0:58 | End card, "Available now" | — |

## Laurel layout

- Laurels are woodcut: two olive sprigs in gilt line (not a photo of a real award asset unless
  the awarding body supplies it and allows use), with the award name in Cinzel capitals and the
  publication in italic small caps beneath.
- A row holds at most **4 laurels**, evenly spaced across the centre 70 % of the frame, 180 px
  wide at 1080p, on a 60 % black band.
- A quote is on screen for at least 3 s. The source is in italic on its own line under the quote.
- Quotes are exact, from published reviews, with the outlet's permission where its policy
  requires it (log it in docs/production/marketing).

## Rules

- **Accolades only in this trailer and on event and sale art that allows them.** Never on base
  store capsules (ART-0321).
- Numbers ("9/10") only if the outlet publishes them and permits their use.
- Localised versions translate the quote only where the outlet published a translation;
  otherwise keep the original with subtitles.
