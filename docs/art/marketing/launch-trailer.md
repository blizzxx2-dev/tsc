## Rules

- No Hour appears in a form past its first. Compline gets a single long shot from a distance, in
  shadow, with no attack shown.
- Chapter III–V footage is captured only from the tagged release build, with the replay IDs
  recorded in the capture list under L-01 to L-06.
- The music drop in beat 9 is the one moment of silence in the trailer. Keep it: it points to
  Compline without showing it.
- 30 s and 15 s cut-downs reuse beats 3, 5–7 and 11–12.
