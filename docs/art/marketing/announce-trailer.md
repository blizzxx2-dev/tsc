## Cut notes

- The hook must read with the sound off: the arrow, the twitch and the COOL stamp carry it.
- Tool shots are framed on the pointer. The instrument is drawn at the tip (ART-0269), so hold each
  shot until the effect fully plays: sparks 0.35 s, sear glow 2 s (cut at 1 s), sealed flash 0.5 s.
- The Malison and Litany beats are the only places where violet or gilt fill the frame. Keep the
  tool montage warm and candle-lit so they land.
- Reduced-flashing is off for capture. Check the export against the three-flashes-per-second rule
  (the Litany burst and phase change are each a single flash).
- Lengths: the beat sheet totals 70 s. To hit 60 s, drop T04 and cut the tools to 1.5 s each.
