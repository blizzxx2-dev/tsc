# Steam autoplay cut — 30 s storyboard (ART-0336)

Steam autoplays the first store video **muted**, in a small player, often from the middle. So this
cut tells its story in pictures and **burned-in woodcut captions**: every beat reads with the sound
off, each caption stays up for at least 1.5 s, and no beat depends on the one before it.

**Captions:** Cinzel capitals, gilt on a black woodcut band (the in-game title-card style,
`band()` + `titleRule()`), bottom third, never over the pointer or a wound. Minimum cap height 42 px
at 1080p, so it stays legible in the 616 px store player (≈ 24 px there). One caption per beat, at
most six words. Captions are localised per store language (LOC-0073); the band leaves 40 % spare width.

Shots come from the [capture list](capture-list.md). Sound (for the unmuted case): the percussion
bed with instrument hits on the cuts.
