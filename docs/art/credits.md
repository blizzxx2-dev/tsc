# Art credits (ART-0352)

Everyone who made art for the game. **This table feeds the in-game credits**:
`src/scenes/creditsData.ts` imports this file and lists every name in the *Credited as* column under
the Art role, in table order. Add a row when a contract is signed (the name exactly as the
contract's credit clause gives it, see docs/production/budget/credit-obligations.csv). Change
it only on the contributor's written request.

| Credited as | Role | Work | Contract / source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kessendorf Workshop | Art direction, procedural art | Shader-drawn flesh, story sets, bosses, VFX, UI kit, tool sprites, cursors, marketing cards | In-house |

## Not credited here

- Type faces are credited with their OFL notices in the type section of the credits (`fontCredits()`).
- Public-domain reference works (Dürer, Bruegel, Bosch, Holbein, Gersdorff) are listed in the art
  bible's reference board, not as contributors.
